We believe that content management shouldn't require a computer science degree. TinaCMS bridges the gap between complex, high-performance code and simple, intuitive content editing. Unlike traditional CMSs that decouple your content from your site's history, Tina keeps everything securely stored in your Git repository while giving you a beautiful visual editor.

### Why Tina CMS?

* **Visual Editing:** Forget dashboard forms. With Tina, you navigate your site just like a visitor. See a typo? Click it. Want to change a banner image? Click it. The changes appear instantly on your screen, exactly as they will look to the world.
* **Git-Backed Security:** Most CMS platforms are black boxes databases that can be hacked or corrupted. Tina stores your content as text files in your secure GitHub repository. You effectively own your data forever, with a complete history of every change ever made.
* **Developer Guardrails:** We define strict "blocks" and components for you. This gives your marketing team the freedom to build pages without the risk of accidentally breaking the layout or brand guidelines.

### The Client Advantage

You get the power of a custom-coded website with the ease of use of a drag-and-drop builder. It empowers your marketing team to move fast—running campaigns, updating copy, and publishing blogs—without waiting for a developer to make simple text changes. It's the perfect balance of autonomy and quality control.
