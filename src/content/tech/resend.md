A contact form that doesn't deliver the email is worse than no contact form at all—it's a lost lead and a damaged reputation. We use Resend to power all transactional emails (contact forms, welcome emails, password resets). It bypasses the unreliable "default" mail functions of standard web servers and routes your mail through a high-reputation delivery network.

### Why Resend?

* **Deliverability First:** Standard PHP `mail()` functions often get flagged as spam by Gmail and Outlook. Resend uses authenticated domains (DKIM/SPF) and clean IP pools to ensure your important business emails land in the "Primary" inbox, not the Spam folder.
* **Modern Developer Experience:** Resend allows us to build emails using React, the same technology we use for your website. This means your emails can be beautiful, branded, and consistent with your site's design—not ugly, plain-text automated messages.
* **Analytics & Tracking:** We can see exactly what happened to every email. Was it delivered? Was it opened? Did it bounce? This visibility is crucial for debugging issues and understanding user engagement.

### The Client Advantage

Reliability. When a potential customer fills out a form on your website, you can be 100% confident that you will receive it. We implement this professional-grade infrastructure to ensure you never miss an opportunity due to a technical glitch.
