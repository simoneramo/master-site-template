We believe you should own your content, not be held hostage by a developer. That's why we integrate user-friendly **Headless CMS solutions like Tina.io**.

### Empower Your Team

TinaCMS gives you a dedicated dashboard to update text, images, and blogs directly on your live site. It feels just like editing a Google Doc.

* **Real-time Previews:** See exactly what your edits look like before you publish.
* **No Code Required:** Change prices, update hours, or post news without touching HTML.
* **Git Backed:** Every change is saved as a "commit" in your code repository, meaning you have a complete history of every edit ever made.

### Ongoing Support

We don't just hand over the keys and run. We offer managed support packages to handle:

* Security updates (though with our static tech, these are rare!).
* New feature additions.
* Regular performance check-ins.
