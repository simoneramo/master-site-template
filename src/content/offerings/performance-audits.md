Is your website losing you business? **53% of mobile users leave sites that take longer than 3 seconds to load.** A slow site drives mobile users away before they even see your offer.

### The Deep Dive

Our Performance Audits aren't just a generic automated scan. We manually inspect your codebase to find:

* **Large Images:** Unoptimized media that eats up bandwidth.
* **Bloated JavaScript:** Unnecessary scripts that freeze the main thread.
* **Render Blocking CSS:** Code that stops your content from appearing instantly.

### What You Get

We provide you with a comprehensive report and **three clear, actionable fixes** to improve your speed and performance immediately. We prioritize "low hanging fruit"—changes that are easy to implement but have a massive impact on your load times and Google Core Web Vitals score.
