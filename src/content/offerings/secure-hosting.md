Security and reliability are non-negotiable. We deploy your site on **Vercel**, an enterprise-grade platform used by companies like Uber and Nike.

### Why Vercel?

Unlike traditional "shared hosting" where your site lives on a single, often overcrowded server, Vercel deploys your website to a **Global Edge Network**. This means your content is replicated across dozens of data centers worldwide. When a user visits your site, they are served from the location physically closest to them, ensuring lightning-fast load times whether they are in Melbourne or New York.

### Unmatched Security

* **DDoS Protection:** Vercel's infrastructure automatically mitigates attacks, ensuring your site stays online even under malicious traffic.
* **SSL by Default:** We provide automatic SSL/TLS encryption (HTTPS), giving your users confidence that their connection is secure.
* **No "Server" to Hack:** Since we serve static assets and serverless functions, there is no traditional operating system or database exposed to the public internet to be exploited.

### Reliability & Uptime

We guarantee **99.9% uptime**. With **Immutable Deployments**, every update you make creates a new, verified version of your site. If something ever looks wrong, we can roll back to the previous version instantly—ensuring your business never faces downtime due to a bad update.
