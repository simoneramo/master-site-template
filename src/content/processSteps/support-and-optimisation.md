A website is like a garden; it needs maintenance to thrive. We don't just hand over the keys and disappear. We offer ongoing support packages to keep your site secure, fast, and growing.

### Continuous Improvement

* **Security Patches:** The web changes fast. We apply critical security updates to your CMS and dependencies so you never have to worry about hacks.
* **Analytics Reports:** We don't just send you a raw data dump. We provide a monthly video summary explaining what the numbers *mean* and what we should improve next month.
* **A/B Testing:** We check what's working (and what's not). We run experiments to improve your conversion rates over time, turning more visitors into customers.
