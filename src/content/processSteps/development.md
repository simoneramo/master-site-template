This is where the magic (and the engineering) happens. We don't use clunky page builders. We write clean, semantic code that Google—and your users—will love.

### Our Tech Stack

* **Semantic HTML & CSS:** We build the foundation of your site using modern standards. This ensures that your content is readable by search engines and accessible to screen readers.
* **Headless Architecture:** We decouple your content from the code using a "Headless CMS". This gives you the freedom to update your content easily without worrying about breaking the site's design.
* **Performance by Default:** We optimize every image, script, and stylesheet. We aim for a "100" score on Google PageSpeed Insights, ensuring your site loads in milliseconds, not seconds.
