Before we write a single line of code, we need to understand exactly **who** we are building for and **why**. Most web projects fail because they jump straight to design without a strategy. We don't make that mistake.

### Our Discovery Process

We treat your business like it's our own. We sit down with you to uncover:

* **The "Why":** What is the core business problem this new site needs to solve? (e.g. More leads? Better brand authority?).
* **Who is Buying:** We build detailed "User Personas" to ensure we are speaking directly to your ideal customer's pain points.
* **Competitor Analysis:** We audit your top 3 competitors to see what they are doing right—and where we can beat them.

### The Blueprint

This isn't just a "chat." You walk away with a **Project Blueprint** that outlines the exact site architecture, key deliverables, and a fixed timeline. This eliminates "scope creep" and ensures we deliver exactly what we promised, on time and on budget.
