Quality is non-negotiable. We don't just "click around" to see if it works. We follow a rigorous 50-point QA checklist to ensure your site is bulletproof.

### What We Test

* **Real Devices:** We test on actual iPhones, Androids, iPads, and Laptops—not just emulators. We ensure your site looks perfect whether it's on a 5-year-old phone or a brand new 4K monitor.
* **Accessibility (WCAG):** We use screen readers (like VoiceOver and NVDA) to navigate your site. If a user with a disability can't use your site, you are losing customers (and potentially breaking the law). We aim for WCAG 2.1 AA compliance.
* **Stress Testing:** We simulate poor network conditions (like 3G) to ensure your site still loads fast for users on the go.
